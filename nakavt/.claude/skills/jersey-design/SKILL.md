# Jersey Design (Dept: Art & Uniforms)

Original kits that read as "pro basketball" without copying any real team.

## Rules
- Pick a **main + trim** color pair (color theory: strong contrast, distinct per team).
- Add an **original short wordmark** (fictional team), a **big number**, and **side
  stripes** — the NBA *format*, an original *identity*.
- Ensure legibility on both the light court and dark backdrops; keep enough hue
  separation between teams so two on-court players never look alike.
- Set the character's `jersey`, `jerseyTrim`, `shorts`, `team`, `number` in
  `src/config.js`. No real team colors+logo combo, no real kit copy.

## Output
Per-team palette + fields, handed to `character-canvas-art` and `originality-review`.
